LOAN POLICY GUIDELINES
======================

1. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   - Minimum age: 18 years
   - Maximum age: 70 years
   - Minimum credit score: 600
   - Stable employment for minimum 2 years
   - Minimum annual income: $30,000

2. LOAN AMOUNT LIMITS
   - Minimum loan amount: $5,000
   - Maximum loan amount: $500,000
   - Loan-to-Value ratio (LTV): Not exceeding 80%

3. INTEREST RATES
   - Prime rate: Base rate + 2-5% (based on creditworthiness)
   - Penalty rate for late payment: +2%
   - Early repayment: No prepayment penalty

4. REPAYMENT TERMS
   - Minimum term: 2 years
   - Maximum term: 30 years
   - Payment frequency: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annual

5. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
   - Valid identification
   - Recent pay stubs (last 3 months)
   - Tax returns (last 2 years)
   - Bank statements (last 3 months)
   - Employment verification letter
   - Property appraisal (if applicable)

6. APPROVAL TIMELINE
   - Document verification: 3-5 business days
   - Credit assessment: 2-3 business days
   - Final approval: 1-2 business days
   - Total expected time: 5-10 business days

7. FEES
   - Application fee: $250
   - Processing fee: 0.5% of loan amount
   - Appraisal fee: $300-$500
   - Title insurance: 0.6% of loan amount
